# Website screenshots — design reference

Captured 2026-07-28 at 1512px wide, viewport height (hero / above-the-fold) unless noted.
Reference material only — nothing here is a build input, and nothing from these files
should be copied verbatim into `site/`.

Two groups: **01–14** are shipped sites from real companies. **15–20** are unbuilt
concept work (Dribbble / Behance shots and a curated gallery) — designs that were never
shipped as products.

## What kept recurring across all 20

1. **One idea above the fold.** Headline, one line of subcopy, one primary button. Nobody stacks three competing messages.
2. **The product appears immediately** — a real screenshot or live widget, usually cropped by the bottom of the viewport so it invites scrolling.
3. **One accent colour, used sparingly.** Modal (green), Ramp (yellow-green) and Mistral (orange) each apply it to about two elements.
4. **Proof sits high.** Logo rows, a live statistic, or customer names land just under the hero — often before any feature copy.
5. **Type does the work.** Very large headline, generous line spacing, no decorative borders. Most of these pages have almost no boxes.

## Not captured, and why

- **Cursor, Perplexity** — the browser extension blocks navigation to those domains.
- **Runway, Godly** — navigation allowed, but screenshots and interaction are denied there.
- **Sierra, Glean** — their hero animations never resolved in the automated session, so every capture was a blank or half-faded frame.
- Individual Dribbble shot pages would not load their full-size image in this setup, which is why the Dribbble entries are search grids rather than single shots.
